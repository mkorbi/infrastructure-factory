Presenting

Keys and modes

The decks run on reveal.js. Everything works from the keyboard, and the speaker view opens in a second window that you drag to the laptop screen while the deck goes to the projector.

→ Space
Next slide. ← goes back, ↓ and ↑ move through vertical slides.
F
Full screen. Esc leaves it.
S
Speaker view: notes, a timer and the next slide, in a window of its own.
O Esc
Overview of every slide. Click one to jump there.
B
Blank the screen while you talk. Press again to come back.
Alt + click
Zoom into part of a slide. Click again to zoom out.
?
Every shortcut.

Each slide has its own address, so a link such as /presentations/the-infrastructure-factory/#/4 opens the deck on slide five. Add ?print-pdf to a deck's address and print from the browser to make your own PDF.

Contribute

Add a presentation

A deck is one folder under presentations/ with a slides.md in it: a front matter block with the title, date, author and a description, then Markdown slides separated by ---, with -- for vertical slides and speaker notes after a line that says Note:. Run node scripts/build-decks.mjs to generate the page, or open a pull request and let the pipeline check it. After the merge the pipeline exports the PDF and publishes the deck here. The details are in CONTRIBUTING.md.
