About

About

The Infrastructure Factory is a working concept by Max Körbächer. This page says who wrote it, what state it is in, where it has been published, and how to follow, contribute and cite it.

Author

Max Körbächer

Max Körbächer serves on the CNCF Governing Board and the Linux Foundation Europe Advisory Board, co-authored "Platform Engineering for Architects", and is Managing Director at Liquid Reply. The views on this site are the author's own.

Status

Working concept, with a changelog

The concept is published to be argued with. Changes are appended here, newest first.

October 2026
Rework into subpages: a pitch on the home page, a full page on the software factory from thirteen sources, a page on how the software, AI and infrastructure factories fit together, three new diagrams, and numbered citations on every figure.
October 2026
Original publication as a single page: definition, evidence, symmetry with the software factory, lineage, the seven-station line, terrain, metrics, twelve factors, prior art and sources.

Contribute

Argue with it

Ideas, counter-examples and your own numbers go to GitHub Discussions. Corrections, sources and building blocks go in as pull requests. The ground rules are in CONTRIBUTING.md: every figure carries a source and a date, projects over products, no pitches, plain language.
