About

About

The Infrastructure Factory is a working concept. This page records its status and changes, and says how to contribute and how to cite it.

Status

Working concept, with a changelog

The concept is published to be argued with. Changes are appended here, newest first.

October 2026
Presentations: talks as reveal.js decks built from Markdown by a pipeline, with speaker notes and PDFs. Page-view counting with GoatCounter, and an llms.txt for AI agents.
October 2026
Rework into subpages: a pitch on the home page, a full page on the software factory from thirteen sources, a page on how the software, AI and infrastructure factories fit together, three new diagrams, and numbered citations on every figure.
October 2026
Original publication as a single page: definition, evidence, symmetry with the software factory, lineage, the seven-station line, terrain, metrics, twelve factors, prior art and sources.

Contribute

Argue with it

Ideas, counter-examples and your own numbers go to GitHub Discussions. Corrections, sources and building blocks go in as pull requests. The ground rules are in CONTRIBUTING.md: every figure carries a source and a date, projects over products, no pitches, plain language.

Cite

How to cite

Körbächer, M. (2026). The Infrastructure Factory. https://infrastructure-factory.com/

The mark

A loop, standing on a floor

The mark draws the thesis. The ring is the software factory loop that everyone draws. The bar is the floor it runs on, the part nobody had built. It reads at favicon size, works in one colour, and follows the theme.

Use it next to the name, as a favicon, or alone where the name is already on the page. Keep the ring and the bar in the proportions of the file; do not add text inside it.
